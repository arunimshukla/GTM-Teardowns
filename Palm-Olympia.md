## Executive Summary

Palm Olympia publishes separate residential and Goldspire pages, floor plans, developer credentials, and monthly construction updates. These are the materials a buyer uses to work out what is offered, who is selling it, and what has been delivered.

The problems appear at those decision points. Similarly branded domains send enquiries to different operators. A payment-plan promotion appears as a graphic with no linked explanation of the offer. A Tower D plan assigns two different figures to balcony area. Developer credentials and construction milestones sit inside images, where a reader cannot search, select, or compare the underlying facts.

The first fixes are to identify the operator beside the enquiry action, publish approved offer terms, and correct the plan. The next step is to give phases, areas, developer credentials, and progress records a consistent text format.

## Project Goals

- Can a buyer identify the operator before sharing contact details?
- Can a buyer verify the terms behind a promotion and compare units and phases?
- Can a buyer retrieve the developer's record, progress evidence, and project disclosures in readable text?

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Official and partner enquiry routes are difficult to distinguish

| Severity | Effort to fix | Type | Target |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **🔴 High** | 🟢 Low | Conversion path | [Official disclaimer](https://palmolympia.com/disclaimer/) and two partner-branded sites, [palmolympia.in](https://palmolympia.in/) and [thepalmolympia.in](https://thepalmolympia.in/) |

**Description**

The .com disclaimer identifies its domain as the official platform. The two .in pages carry agent or channel-partner disclosures and use similar project branding. The enquiry numbers differ across the surfaces. The disclosures identify how the operators describe themselves. Partner authorisation remains outside the public check.

A buyer can recognise the project and still be unsure which operator will receive the enquiry.

**Recommendations**

1. Identify the enquiry operator beside every contact action and link to the official domain where applicable.
2. Publish a directory of partners whose authorisation has been verified and agree a consistent disclosure for their forms.

### 2. The payment-plan promotion has no linked explanation of the terms

| Severity | Effort to fix | Type | Target |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **🔴 High** | 🟢 Low | Commercial evidence | [Homepage promotion](https://palmolympia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/POPUP.jpg-2-1517x2048.jpeg), [residential page](https://palmolympia.com/palm-olympia/), and [Plans](https://palmolympia.com/plans/) |

**Description**

The homepage campaign graphic advertises 40% now and 60% on possession alongside a ₹1.92 crore figure for 3 and 4 BHK units. It marks terms as applicable. The promotion does not link to a text explanation of eligible units, price inclusions, payment triggers, or validity.

The figure is recorded as displayed on the graphic. Current inventory, final prices, and eligibility require sales confirmation.

**Recommendations**

1. Publish dated, sales-approved terms alongside the promotion and link directly to them from the creative.
2. State eligibility, the price basis, additional charges, payment triggers, expiry, and the relevant enquiry route.

### 3. The Tower D plan labels two figures as balcony area

| Severity | Effort to fix | Type | Target |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **🔴 High** | 🟢 Low | Product evidence | [Tower D 2 BHK plus two toilets and study plan](https://palmolympia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/PO-Floor-Plans-54.jpg) on the [Plans page](https://palmolympia.com/plans/) |

**Description**

The plan labels both 677.23 sq ft and 138.81 sq ft as balcony area. It separately lists 1,182 sq ft as super area and 902.46 sq ft as built-up area. The duplicate label prevents a buyer from knowing what the first figure represents from the plan alone.

**Recommendations**

1. Check the area schedule with the architect and replace the asset once the intended label is confirmed.
2. Publish the approved area categories and figures as text alongside the plan. Check brochures and partner copies that reuse it.

### 4. The developer record is available only in presentation graphics

| Severity | Effort to fix | Type | Target |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **🔴 High** | 🟠 Medium | Evidence | [Sam India page](https://palmolympia.com/sam-india-2/) |

**Description**

The Sam India page presents the company story and project record in background-image sections. The copy in those panels is absent from the page text. The final call to action promises a visit to the developer website. In the original review it opened a download form, and the recheck found the same link connected to the form markup.

A buyer researching delivered projects can read the graphics visually, but cannot search or select the evidence or follow the promised link to the developer.

**Recommendations**

1. Publish the developer story, delivered-project names, and relevant dates as text alongside the photographs.
2. Point the button to the verified corporate website or label the download for what it is.

### 5. The collections and phases have no shared comparison

| Severity | Effort to fix | Type | Target |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **🟠 Medium** | 🟠 Medium | Product positioning | [Goldspire](https://palmolympia.com/goldspire/), [residential page](https://palmolympia.com/palm-olympia/), and [Plans](https://palmolympia.com/plans/) |

**Description**

Goldspire presents Club Harmonie. The residential page promotes Club Meridian. Plans splits the master plans by phase. The pages do not give a buyer one way to connect the collection, phase, tower, configuration, and associated club access.

**Recommendations**

1. Publish one approved comparison by phase and tower, including area categories, configurations, relevant registrations, and club entitlements.
2. Link to it from both collection pages and distinguish fixed entitlements from current availability.

### 6. Construction milestones are published in images without a text record

| Severity | Effort to fix | Type | Target |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **🟠 Medium** | 🟠 Medium | Evidence | [Construction archive](https://palmolympia.com/construction/) and [October update](https://palmolympia.com/october-2026/) |

**Description**

The October update presents tower milestones in graphics. The original review also found older reference photographs identified by dates in the images. The page text does not give the tower, stage, and reporting date together. A buyer comparing progress month to month has to open and read each graphic.

**Recommendations**

1. Add a dated table to every update with the tower, stage reached, change since the previous period, and evidence image.
2. Label reference photographs separately and link the latest dated update from the homepage.

### 7. The site-visit phone input has no visible prompt

| Severity | Effort to fix | Type | Target |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **🟠 Medium** | 🟢 Low | Conversion path | [Book a Site Visit form](https://palmolympia.com/) on the homepage |

**Description**

The telephone input sits beside the country selector without a visible phone prompt. The original review recorded 12 px input type and no explicit autofill purposes on the relevant fields. The source recheck confirms the missing visible prompt.

**Recommendations**

1. Give the country selector and phone field persistent labels. Declare the input purposes and add a clear example where useful.
2. Check readability and form completion at narrow widths before releasing the shared form.

### 8. The project disclosures are difficult to scan in the footer

| Severity | Effort to fix | Type | Target |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **🟠 Medium** | 🟢 Low | Project evidence | Phase 2 and Phase 3 disclosures in the [homepage footer](https://palmolympia.com/) |

**Description**

The footer publishes project registration and collection information. In the original desktop check, the disclosure copy measured 9 px and the phase labels 10 px. A buyer has to distinguish the two phases in small type during a verification task.

**Recommendations**

1. Use readable disclosure type and a distinct block for each phase. Link to the applicable official registration record.
2. Check each published identifier against the approved source before changing the presentation.

## Appendix C: Confidence and Limitations

This is a project-side review of the public website. Offer eligibility, partner authorisation, approved areas, delivery status, and legal compliance were outside the evidence available. The review uses public material and does not infer advertising performance, form completion, autofill outcomes, or mobile device timings.
